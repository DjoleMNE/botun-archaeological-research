The disclaimer must remain legible near the top of EVERY page including 404. A shorter reminder must also remain in the footer. This project is entirely AI-produced at Đorđe Vukčević's request, uses publicly available sources, and has received no human verification of its results, including from Đorđe. Do not suggest that NASA, Copernicus, USGS or a national authority validated project findings merely because their public data were used.

## Readability
- Explain every numeric result in one simple sentence, including what it does *not* mean.
- Prefer "mjerenja" over "otkrivene strukture", "početna provjera kvaliteta" over unexplained QA jargon, and "model visine zemljišta" over bare acronyms.
- Distinguish data obtained, initial processing, and human-confirmed discoveries; there are no confirmed discoveries.
- Avoid unsupported precision or confidence ratings. No site-level geolocation or confidential archaeological leads.
- Cite data provider, title and acquisition dates when introducing new numbers.
- UI, alt text, map popups, controls, error states, captions and figure labels must be Montenegrin Latin.
- Scientific figures with embedded English labels need localization prior to site use.
